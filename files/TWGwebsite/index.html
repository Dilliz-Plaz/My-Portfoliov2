The Weird Party

Policies

Homelessness

The homeless population in Canada is roughly 65,000 people as of the end of 2025; this level is 59% higher than counts from 2020–2022, showing a significant rise since the pandemic. 65,000 people may not be much compared to the overall population of 39.19 million people, but even then, it is still an issue that needs to be addressed, instead of being pushed off like previous years. Here at TWP, we can and will offer grants to private sectors like Forestry, to provide open programs where, if the homeless want help, they can find guaranteed housing, food, pay and rehab.

Housing

Housing in Canada is a big issue that we at TWP would like to fix. According to a study done in 2024, which consisted of a little over 1000 Canadians, about 80% of people agreed that “Owning a home in Canada is now only for the rich,” and 70% of people “have given up on ever owning a home.” To lower housing costs across Canada, we will give grants to Provinces so they can build low-income apartments and more stable housing, like condos and large family homes.

Public Relations

Canada needs alliances. We have learned from previous experiences that we need to broaden our trading range to the European world. TWP will lead Canada to new trade deals with our closest allies, bringing lower prices for everyday items to Canadians. Although our current government is working towards this, we at TWP would like to push that even further.

Immigration

Immigration in Canada is going up every day, exceeding 400,000 people every year; at least 8.3 million people from all over the world have legally immigrated to Canada, that being 23% of Canada's population. As much as we love our friends around the world, it has been voiced by our younger generation that finding jobs has become extremely difficult. As much as we want to work on public relations, we need to put Canada first. This would still mean keeping student exchange and refugee programs open.

Taxing

As of 2026, taxes like GST, PST, and HST are still going up, as well as property tax, which, for the less fortunate, working class and middle class, is a big issue. To solve this problem, we will lower taxes for low-income workers and tax the 1% more. Instead of taxing the rich based on what they have on hand and in their accounts, we will tax their net worth, stocks, and exit tax.

Healthcare

We are aware of the lack of good healthcare, like the hours of waiting in the emergency room lobby, with there only being roughly 0.24–0.26% of the total population who are doctors. This means that for every 1,000 Canadians, about 2 to 2.5 are doctors. So, we have come up with an idea to make healthcare more accessible and to advertise it. We will make student loans for doctors lower to make it easier for them to pay them off, so that they can be paid sooner. That will help increase healthcare staff around the country and should lower the wait time in hospitals and clinics. Alongside student loan forgiveness, we will also give grants to Provinces to build more medical schools and fund more residency positions.
